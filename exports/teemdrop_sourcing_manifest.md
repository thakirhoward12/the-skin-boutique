## Critical Cost & Regulatory Guardrails (SARS 2026 Compliance)

To protect our margins, I need a **Total Landed Cost** quote per product. Please factor in the following South African specificities:

### 1. The VAT & Duty Formula

SARS calculates VAT on the "Added Tax Value" (ATV). Here is the exact formula I need you to price against:

```
Customs Value  = FOB Price (USD) × Exchange Rate (ZAR/USD)
Duty           = Customs Value × Duty Rate (typically 20% for HS 3304)
ATV            = Customs Value + Freight + Insurance + Duty
VAT            = ATV × 15%
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Total Landed   = Customs Value + Freight + Insurance + Duty + VAT
```

**Example:** A $15 serum at R19.50/USD:
- Customs Value = $15 × 19.50 = R292.50
- Duty (20%) = R58.50
- ATV = R292.50 + R45 (freight) + R5 (insurance) + R58.50 = R401.50
- VAT (15%) = R60.23
- **Total Landed = R461.73**

Please ensure your shipping partner provides a **clean commercial invoice** with accurate FOB values to avoid over-valuation by customs.

### 2. HS Code Classification

Skincare products fall under **HS 3304** (Beauty or make-up preparations and preparations for the care of the skin) at approximately **20% MFN duty**. Please confirm HS code classification for each product category in our manifest so we can verify duty rates.

### 3. Small-Parcel Regulations (2026)

As of the latest SARS updates, small retail parcels face increased scrutiny. Please prioritise shipping lines (e.g., Buffalo, specialised K-Beauty lines) that offer **established clearance channels** to minimise per-parcel delays and ad hoc duty assessments.

### 4. Port Health & SAHPRA Compliance

Skincare is a "high-risk" category for SA customs. Please confirm that your sources provide products with:
- Valid **INCI Ingredient Lists** in English on all packaging
- No banned substances per SAHPRA's cosmetic ingredient schedule
- Proper batch/lot numbers for traceability

Failure to comply risks Port Health seizures and lab testing fees of approximately **R1,500 per incident** — which would destroy our margin on that order entirely.
